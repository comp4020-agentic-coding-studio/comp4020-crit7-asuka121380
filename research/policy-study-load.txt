Clause 2: "Study period 1: is 1 January to 30 June including Summer Session, First Semester and Autumn Session"; "study period 2: is 1 July to 31 December, including Winter Session, Second Semester and Spring Session."
Clause 12: "A standard study load is 24 units in a standard period."
Clause 13: undergraduates "wishing to enrol in more than 24 units but not more than 30 units in a single study period" must meet overload criteria (units passed, averages).
Clause 14: undergraduates "wishing to enrol in more than 30 units but not more than 36 units in a single study period" must meet stricter criteria.
Clause 19: sessions that do not overlap the semester are not counted as overloading, provided the student takes only 24 units in the semester.
Clause 20: "Students are not given permission to enrol in more than 36 units in a study period."

How the planner uses this: more than 24 units in one study period is a warning (possible with
overload approval); more than 36 is refused (clause 20).
