Requirements
The INSY major requires the completion of 48 units, of which:
A maximum of 18 units may come from completion of 1000-level courses
A minimum of 18 units must come from completion of 3000 and 4000-level courses
18 units from completion of the following compulsory courses:
[COMP2620] Logic
[COMP3620] Artificial Intelligence
[COMP3670] Introduction to Machine Learning
A minimum of 12 units from completion of Intelligent Systems courses from the following list:
[COMP3242] Deep Learning
[COMP3600] Algorithms
[COMP4620] Advanced Topics in Artificial Intelligence
[COMP4670] Statistical Machine Learning
[COMP4680] Advanced Topics in Machine Learning
[COMP4691] Optimisation
A maximum of 18 units from completion of courses from the following list:
[COMP4528] Computer Vision
[COMP4610] Computer Graphics
[COMP4650] Document Analysis
This Major is incompatible with the Artificial Intelligence Specialisation and the Machine Learning Specialisation and the Intelligent Systems Specialisation.
